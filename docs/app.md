## Map
- Zoom numbers are "classic" = MapLibre zoom + 1. Min 3, max 21. Pins, stacks and place data are redrawn / loaded only on `moveend` (60 / 120 ms after), never during a pinch or drag; markers fade in (.18 s) and out (.15 s). View saved in `jk_view`.
- Data: squares `places/<x>_<y>.json` only at classic zoom ≥ 13 (≥ 11 when a filter is on), max 6 (9 big screen; 9 / 13 with a filter below 13), nearest the middle first, 1200 rows per portion; from zoom 15 the small squares `places/14/…`.
- Pins: below 13 nothing except saved places · 13–14 a few calm stacks (cell 170 / 140 px, max 8 phone / 14 big screen, 150×70 px room between them, lone places hidden unless fewer than 3 stacks; tap = zoom +2) · ≥ 15 rating-pill pins, best first, caps 18 / 30 / 48 / 70 / 100 · saved places always shown, never stacked (gold pill; Favorites red) · team / visitor places always their own pin.
- Group badge = flat count pill (green body, cream circle, gold ring). Pin picture by food type (`JK.pinui.kind`: bakery, noodles, sushi, grill, dumplings, else the category). Small Halal / Vegan badges on pins and groups.
- Zoom buttons + / − (`#zstep`, right side above the locate button) and a quiet note `#znote` ("Zoom in closer to see restaurants.") while places are hidden. No zoom circle (removed 2026-11-17: it blocked pinches).
- Dark map = OpenFreeMap positron recoloured (`DARK` in maplib.js): blue water, green parks, lighter roads, gold motorways, bright labels.
- Team / visitor places: `npLoad()` in places.js reads the view `new_places_public` (read only, by map box, from zoom 11), ids `np_<n>`. Shown: team `verified`, or ≥ 3 yes and more yes than no; not yet confirmed ones only from zoom 16 or to the phone that added them (`jk_npmine`). Halal / Vegan come from the database (the 5 Kyoto halal places, `database/12-kyoto-halal.sql`).

## Place card
- Always 92 dvh high; small = `translateY(26dvh)`, full = none. It follows the finger 1:1 (class `drag`), snaps full / small / closed, a flick works. Grab it by the bar or anywhere in the top 72 px. Sheets and pop-ups follow the finger too (top 64 px or the bar) and close on 90 px or a flick.
- Content: rating, pills (price range, food type, Halal / Vegan), swipe row (Navigate, Save to a list, Write a review, Share, Report wrong info), tabs Details (Address – one tap copies it, Phone, Opening hours, Website) / Reviews / Menu photos. A visitor-added place also shows its outside photo and, until confirmed, "Is this place really here?" Yes / No (`new_place_votes`, `jk_npvoted`).
- Navigate sheet: only the map apps that fit the phone + Copy address / Copy coordinates (only there). Report sheet: Halal / Vegan / food (one empty typing field, list only while typing) / closed; one answer per phone (database).

## Filters, search, List
- Filter state is shared (`JK.filters`): country, Halal (marked halal), Some halal options, Vegan / vegetarian, Favorites, a list from My places, Top rated (≥ 4 stars), Price = a bar with 4 stops (up to Cheap / Mid / High / Any price; levels 1–4 come from reviews, `JK.ratings.all()` loads every reviewed place). The money follows the country (country filter, else the country in the middle of the map; `JK.price` in reviews.js: Japan ¥ 1,500 / 4,000 / 10,000 · Korea ₩ 13,000 / 30,000 / 80,000 · China ¥ 50 / 150 / 400), written the local way when the app is in that language (1,500円 · 13,000원 · 50元), the number counts while you slide, and the chip follows when you travel to another country (`JK.filters.follow`). Review sheet, card and List use the PLACE's country, Open now (`js/hours.js`: OSM opening_hours, local time of the country, unclear = not open), categories, food text, Japanese / Korean / Chinese food. A filter that is ON shows as a number on the filter button + one removable chip per filter (and Reset) at the start of the chip row.
- Food type (`js/food.js`): typing field with typo help (`fold` + `near`; fewer than 4 hits → typo pass) in any language (`lang/foods.json`). Visitor reports accept only the fixed list of 38 kinds (`food:<slug>`, database rule). Used in the report sheet, the Filters food box and the search bar (food-type suggestions on top; tap = filter food).
- Search (`js/search.js`): name, area, food type (all languages), address; typos forgiven; city jump (8 cities + Nominatim on Enter, 30-day cache `jk_c_city_*`).
- List screen (`#listscr`): same filters as the map (chips row `#lcats`, filter button `data-open-filters`). Places = loaded places inside the MIDDLE 60 % × 60 % of the map view, at most 150 (nearest the middle; shown "150+"), 30 rows at a time + "Show 30 more" (light on purpose: some phones crash); all saved places when Favorites / a list is on. Sorts Nearest first · Top rated · Cheapest first · Most expensive first (`jk_lsort`; price and rating come from visitors' reviews, places without go last). Tap a row = opens it on the map. A list from My places also opens the List screen.

## Add a restaurant (`js/addplace.js`, Menu > Add a restaurant)
- Map zooms to classic 17. Step 1: a slim bar (hint + "Add here") and a plain + in the middle of the free map. Step 2 (spot kept in `st.spot`, Back returns to aiming): name, kind, food type (optional, from the food list), Halal / Vegan / Vegetarian, photo of the outside (required; `JK.sb.shrink` + `upload("newplace")`), Send = `JK.sb.postId("new_places", …)`.
- The database refuses: no photo, same name within 15 m, > 5 a day per phone, outside Japan / Korea / China. The adder sees the place at once; others from classic zoom 16; after 3 yes it shows like any place.

- Signed in: the "Moon gate" profile card replaces Sign in / Create account; tap = profile page `#profile`. Name / email from `jk_user` (`{name,email}`). Accounts are not live; `JKAuth.signIn(user)` / `signOut()` or a Supabase session (`sb-…-auth-token`) turns it on. Change name / email / password, Delete account, Sign in / Create account, Jakorchi Plus, Add a tip, Useful phrases, About = "Coming soon" popups. Back button closes a popup (one history step).
- Short phones (≤ 680 px high): tighter spacing, no scroll on the front page. Pinch / double-tap zoom is OFF everywhere except the map (`maximum-scale=1`, `touch-action`, `gesturestart` block in app.js). Top bar and settings back button sit `--shift` lower than the safe area (iOS blurs the strip under the clock).
