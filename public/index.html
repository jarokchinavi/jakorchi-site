About & privacy

Jakorchi is a free food guide for travelers in Japan, Korea and China. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Saved on your phone only

Your language, favorites, lists and tips stay on this phone. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What you send

Reviews, photos, reports and places you add are saved in our database and shown to everyone. A random code from your phone is saved with them to stop spam. It is not shown to anyone and is not linked to your name. Location data is removed from photos.

Your location

Your location is used on your phone to show where you are and how far places are. We do not save it.

Other services

The map, search, fonts and database come from other services: OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap, Photon, Google Fonts, Supabase and Cloudflare. Like every website, they can see your internet address (IP).

Remove something

A photo can be reported with the button on the photo; after two reports it is hidden. To have your own review removed, contact us.
